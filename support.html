01

Contact us

Email summarisewithclarity@proton.me. We aim to reply within two working days.

What to include

Your device model and iOS version (Settings › General › About), the Clarity version (Settings › About in the app), the reading level you chose, and what happened. Please don't send us the document itself - we can almost always help without it, and we would rather not receive your contracts.

02

What you need to run Clarity

Clarity summarises using Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device rather than on a server. That means it needs:

  • A device that supports Apple Intelligence
  • iOS 26 or later
  • Apple Intelligence switched on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri
  • Enough free storage for the on-device model to finish downloading

If Apple Intelligence is off or still downloading, Clarity will tell you so rather than failing quietly.

03

"Couldn't summarise this content"

This means Apple's on-device safety check declined the document. It is not a judgement about you, and the document has not been sent anywhere - the check runs on your phone, before any summary is written.

What helps, in order:

  • Try again. The check is not perfectly consistent; a second attempt often succeeds.
  • Change the reading level. A document declined at one level frequently works at another.
  • Try a shorter section. Scanning fewer pages sometimes gets through.

Some documents will not go through at all

Documents dealing with illness, death, or personal financial hardship are the most likely to be declined, because those are subjects the safety check treats cautiously. We cannot override it - it is part of iOS, not part of Clarity. We would rather say so plainly than leave you retrying forever.

04

Summaries are slow

A summary usually takes somewhere between 15 seconds and a minute or so. Longer documents, and the more detailed reading levels, take longer.

This is the cost of running on your phone instead of a server: there is no data centre doing the work, just your device. Keeping Clarity in the foreground while it works is the single most effective thing you can do, since iOS gives background apps less processing time. A very long document, a hot phone, or Low Power Mode will all slow it down.

05

Reading levels

Clarity has five, from Explain like I'm five to Lawyer. Set the default in Settings, or change any individual summary from its own screen - the figures stay the same, only the language changes.

Detailed and Lawyer require Clarity Pro. The other three are free.

Switching a document to a level you have used before is instant, because it was saved. A level you have not used yet has to be generated, which takes as long as the first summary did.

06

Free summaries and Clarity Pro

The free tier includes three summaries a month and up to eight pages per document. The month runs from your first summary, not from the 1st.

"I have fewer summaries than I expected"

Your allowance belongs to your Apple ID, not to a single device. If you use Clarity on more than one device, they share one pool of three - they do not get three each. Reinstalling does not reset it either. This is deliberate.

Clarity Pro removes the monthly limit and the page limit, and unlocks all five reading levels. Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Deleting your Clarity account does not cancel a subscription.

07

Sync, sign-in and your name

Signing in with Apple is optional. Without it, Clarity works fully and your documents stay on the one device. Signing in syncs your documents and some settings through your own private iCloud.

If a document has not appeared on your other device, check that both are signed into the same Apple ID, that both have iCloud Drive enabled, and that both have a working connection. First sync on a new device can take several minutes.

Clarity shows "Signed in" instead of my name

Apple sends an app your name and email only on your first-ever sign-in. If you previously deleted your Clarity account, that first sign-in has already happened, so there is nothing left for us to show.

To fix it: go to Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple › Clarity and choose Stop using Apple ID. The next sign-in then counts as a first sign-in again, and your name comes back.

08

Deleting your account

Account › Delete account, in the app. This permanently removes your account and every scan and summary stored with it, on the device and in iCloud. It cannot be undone.

Two things deletion does not do, both outside our control:

  • It does not cancel a Clarity Pro subscription - cancel that in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • It does not remove Clarity from Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple. Remove it there yourself if you want a completely clean slate. Doing so is also what restores your name if you sign up again.
09

Privacy and your documents

Your documents are summarised on your device and are never sent to us or to any third-party server. If you sign in, they sync to your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple ID can open. We never hold a copy.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy, and the terms you agree to by using Clarity are Apple's Standard End User Licence Agreement, which is what the app itself links to.

10

Accuracy, and what Clarity is not

Clarity summarises with AI and can make mistakes. It can miss a term, misread a figure, or describe something in a way that does not quite match the original.

Clarity is not legal advice

It is a reading aid, not a lawyer. Always read the original document, and consult a qualified solicitor before you sign, rely on, or act on anything Clarity tells you. If a summary and the document disagree, the document is what counts.

Very long documents are shortened before summarising, so the summary may not cover every clause. The flagged clauses are a prompt to look closer, not a complete list of everything that matters.
