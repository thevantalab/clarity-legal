01

What Clarity is

Clarity is an app that helps you understand legal documents - leases, contracts, NDAs, and similar paperwork - by scanning or importing them and generating a plain-English summary at the level of detail you choose.

This policy explains what happens to your documents, and any other information, when you use Clarity.

02

Your documents & how they're processed

When you scan or import a document, Clarity reads the text using your device's camera and Apple's built-in text recognition - on your device.

Your summary is then written by Apple's on-device AI (part of Apple Intelligence), which runs the language model locally, using your device's own hardware.

Your document's content is never transmitted to Clarity, to any server we operate, or to any third-party AI service. We don't run servers that process your documents - so there's nowhere for that content to go except your own device.

03

Where your data lives

By default, your scanned documents, extracted text, and generated summaries are stored only on your device, in Clarity's private app storage.

If you choose to sign in with your Apple ID, your documents and settings can optionally sync across your own devices through Apple's iCloud - specifically, your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple ID can access. This is entirely your choice: Clarity works fully without ever signing in.

We never hold a copy ourselves

Whether or not you're signed in, Clarity itself never sees, accesses, or stores a copy of your documents. When iCloud sync is enabled, your data sits in your personal iCloud account, governed by Apple's own privacy practices - see Apple's Privacy Policy for how iCloud handles your data.

04

Permissions we ask for

  • Camera - to scan physical documents. Used only to capture and read the pages you point it at; nothing is recorded or stored beyond the pages you choose to keep.
  • Photo library - only if you choose to import an existing photo of a document instead of using the camera.

Neither permission gives Clarity a live or ongoing feed of your camera or photo library - only what you actively select to scan or import.

05

Accounts, Sign in with Apple & purchases

Signing in is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. Depending on the choices you make when signing in, we may receive a unique identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and/or email address - Apple lets you hide your real email behind a private relay address instead, if you'd rather.

Clarity Pro is bought through the App Store, and your payment is handled entirely by Apple - Clarity never sees or stores your card details. The App Store confirms your purchase to the app on your device, which is how Pro is unlocked. That check does not pass through any server of ours, and buying Pro does not require a Clarity account. The same applies to a Clarity Pro subscription bought in an earlier version of the app. Apple gives us sales figures that do not identify you.

06

What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or behavioural tracking that profiles you.
  • We don't use advertising or third-party tracking technology.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your documents or personal information with anyone - we have nothing to sell, since we never hold it.
  • No one at Clarity reads, reviews, or has access to your documents. Ever.

One honest caveat: if you've opted into sharing analytics with app developers under your iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymised crash/diagnostic data with us for that reason alone. That's controlled entirely by your own iOS Settings, not by anything Clarity does - and it never includes your document content.

07

Your choices & control

  • Delete anytime: Settings → Data & privacy → Delete all scans & summaries removes everything from your device, and from iCloud too if you're signed in.
  • Exporting or sharing is up to you: when you export a PDF or back up your documents, that file only goes wherever you choose to send it - AirDrop, Messages, Files, and so on. Clarity doesn't see or keep a copy of what you share.
  • Sign out anytime from Settings - this doesn't delete the documents already on your device.

Because we don't hold your data ourselves, most of the rights you'd expect to formally request from a company - access, correction, deletion, export - are already fully in your hands, on your own device, without needing to ask us for anything.

08

Children's privacy

Clarity isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age where you live). In practice, Clarity doesn't collect meaningful personal information from anyone at all - but if you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, please contact us and we'll help.

09

We may update this policy occasionally - for example, if we add a new feature that changes how data is handled. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page whenever we do. Continuing to use Clarity after a change means you accept the updated policy.
